The ritual

Tea is not a drink we hurry.
It is a pause we keep.

Heat the water just shy of a boil. Measure a heaping spoon of leaf. Watch the colour unfurl. Three slow minutes — and the day softens. Every blend we make is built to honour that quiet.

01 · Measure

One rounded teaspoon per cup of fresh, soft water.

02 · Steep

Let the leaves open fully — never rushed, never bitter.

03 · Linger

Pour, breathe, and let the morning settle around you.

About Verdant & Vale

Founded on a single belief — that good tea is a small, daily act of kindness to yourself.

What began as a kitchen-table experiment with a cracked teapot has grown into a quiet little house of tea. We're still a small team, still tasting everything ourselves, and still convinced that the right cup can change the shape of a day.
