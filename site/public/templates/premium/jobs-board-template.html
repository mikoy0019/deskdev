Our story

Hiring should feel like a match, not a maze

We started Roleplate after sending 300 applications between us and hearing back from nine. The job market wasn't broken because of talent — it was broken because of noise, opacity, and ghosting.

So we built a marketplace where pay is public, every company is vetted, and a reply rate is a number you can see before you apply. The result: faster offers for candidates and better hires for teams.
