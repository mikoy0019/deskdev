How It Goes

CONSULT → DESIGN → ink

01

Consult

We sit down — in person or over a call — to map your idea, placement, and budget. Bring references, scars, stories. Deposit secures your slot.

02

Design

Your artist draws a one-off piece sized to your body. One round of refinements is included before we ever pick up a machine.

03

Ink

Single-use needles, hospital-grade station, and as many sessions as the piece needs. You leave with aftercare and a healed-photo invite.

Before You Book

COMMON questions

Limited walk-ins on Thursdays with whichever artist has space. Everything else is custom and booked through a consult.

A non-refundable $80 deposit holds your design slot and is applied to your final session total. Reschedule once with 48 hours' notice.

Always. We share the drawing before your appointment and include one round of refinements. Bigger changes are re-quoted.

18+ with a valid government photo ID, no exceptions. We can't tattoo anyone intoxicated or unwell — for your safety and the art.

Sleep well, eat beforehand, hydrate, and skip alcohol for 24 hours. Wear comfortable clothes that expose the area. Snacks welcome.
