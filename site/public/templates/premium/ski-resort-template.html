05 — Our Story

Half a century, one obsession: the perfect turn.

Alpenfrost began in 1971 with a single rope-tow and a family who believed the mountain belonged to everyone. Five decades on, we still groom by hand at dawn and still believe a good day on snow can change a life.

Today we run on 100% renewable hydro power, plant a tree for every season pass, and protect 4,000 hectares of alpine wilderness — so the next generation gets their turns too.
