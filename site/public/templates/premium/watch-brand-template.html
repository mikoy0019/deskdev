— The Maison —

Founded in 1894. Independent ever since.

AETHERWERK was born in a single workshop above a Geneva clocktower, where a master and one apprentice resolved to build no more than a hundred watches a year. Five generations later, we still don't. We answer to no shareholder, no quarterly forecast — only to the next escapement.

Twenty-seven hands. One observatory. A patience that has outlasted three empires and every fashion in between.

— Considered Questions —

Before you commit.

Because we cap production at one hundred watches per year, current references carry a wait of 9 to 18 months. Bespoke commissions are quoted individually.

Every calibre is designed, machined, finished, assembled and regulated under our own roof in Geneva. Only the sapphire crystal and strap leather are sourced from long-standing partners.

A full service every five to seven years, carried out by the original maison. Each watch is returned with a renewed certificate and an updated provenance archive — for life.

No piece leaves the maison without a conversation. We invite collectors to our salon or arrange a private viewing in your city. The watch deserves an introduction.
