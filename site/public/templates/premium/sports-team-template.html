Matchday Info

FAQ

How do I buy matchday tickets? +

Tickets go on general sale ten days before each fixture. Members get a 72-hour priority window. Buy securely through the Tickets button at the top of the page.

Is the Foundry Stadium family friendly? +

Absolutely. The East Stand is a designated family zone with discounted junior tickets, baby-change facilities, and a half-time activity area for under-12s.

Can I upgrade my membership mid-season? +

Yes — upgrade any time from your member account and only pay the prorated difference. New benefits unlock immediately.

How do I get to the stadium? +

The Foundry is a 12-minute walk from Vale Central station. Matchday shuttle buses run from the city ring road, and accessible parking can be booked in advance.
