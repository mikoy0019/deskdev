About Margin & Marrow

A room that smells of paper and rain.

We opened on Marrowgate Lane with three folding tables and a cat named Index. Today we're a proper shop and a small press, printing a handful of titles a year we couldn't bear to leave unpublished. The cat is still here. He has opinions.

Come for a recommendation, stay for the back room. We make tea, we argue about endings, and we'll never make you feel foolish for what you love to read.
