The Atelier

Made by hand, without hurry.

Founded in 2014 on a narrow street in the Marais, Séraphine began with a single tailor and a refusal to rush. Today, eleven artisans cut, drape and finish every piece under one roof.

We work only in natural fibres, dye in small lots, and produce nothing we cannot stand behind for a decade. Slowness is our discipline — and our luxury.

Shipping & Care

We ship worldwide. European orders arrive within 2–4 working days; international orders within 5–9. Each piece leaves the atelier wrapped in unbleached cotton and a numbered card of authenticity.

Unworn pieces may be returned within 30 days for a full refund or exchange. Made-to-measure and final-sale items are exempt. Return shipping within the EU is complimentary.

Most pieces are best cold hand-washed or dry-cleaned, then laid flat to dry. Linen softens beautifully with wear; wool benefits from rest between wears. A care card accompanies every garment.

Yes. Our atelier accepts a limited number of made-to-measure commissions each season, scheduled by appointment in Paris or remotely via guided measurement. Lead time is approximately six weeks.
