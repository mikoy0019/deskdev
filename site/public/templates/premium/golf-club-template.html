A Century in the Making

A landscape shaped by time, tradition & care.

Carved into Berkshire's ancient parkland in 1924, Wexford Vale was conceived not merely as a course, but as a sanctuary. Generations of members have walked these fairways — through morning mist, golden afternoons, and the long shadows of midsummer evenings.

Today the club remains proudly private: a place of unhurried excellence, where every detail is considered and every season is honoured.

Membership is by proposal and second from two existing members. Prospective members are warmly invited to visit, play the course and dine before any application proceeds to the committee.

To preserve the character of the club, membership is capped. Most categories have availability; Legacy tenure may carry a short waiting period. The secretary will advise on current openings.
