Our story

Built by pet people, for pet people.

Brightpaw opened its doors in 2009 with one stubborn idea: that going to the vet shouldn't feel scary. Today our neighborhood clinic pairs that warmth with the diagnostics, dentistry and surgery of a full hospital.

We're locally owned, never corporate — which means decisions are made by the doctors who actually know your pet's name (and their favorite snack).
