FAQ

Questions, answered plainly

Do you work with my existing accounting software? +

Yes. We work in QuickBooks, Xero and most modern platforms. If you're not on one yet, we'll set you up on the right fit during onboarding — at no extra cost.

How far behind can my books be? +

As far as it takes. We've untangled clients who hadn't reconciled in years. We quote catch-up work separately and clearly before any commitment.

Are taxes included in the monthly price? +

Annual business filing is included on every plan. Personal returns and multi-state complexity are available as add-ons, always quoted up front — never a surprise.

Is there a long-term contract? +

No lock-in. Our plans are month-to-month — we'd rather earn your business every month than trap you in a contract.

What if I get audited? +

We've got you. Audit support and IRS correspondence are included on Growth and Advisory plans, and available as an add-on for Essentials.
