The Atelier

Grown slowly,

arranged by hand.

Fleuriste began as a single market stall and a quiet obsession with seasonality. Today our atelier works with a small circle of regenerative British growers, cutting at dawn so every stem reaches you at its peak.

No imported uniformity, no foam, no waste — only flowers with a sense of place, tied by hands that know them.

Good to know

Frequently asked

Which areas do you deliver to?

+

We offer same-day hand delivery across the city centre and surrounding boroughs, and next-day courier delivery nationwide. Enter your postcode at checkout to confirm.

How long will my flowers last?

+

Every bouquet comes with a freshness guarantee of at least seven days. Each delivery includes care notes — trim, refresh the water, and they'll thank you for it.

Can I pause or cancel a subscription?

+

Anytime, no questions asked. Manage, skip, pause or cancel from your account up to 48 hours before your next dispatch.

Do you take on wedding florals?

+

We do — a limited number each season. Send us your date and vision via the enquiry form and our lead florist will arrange a consultation.
