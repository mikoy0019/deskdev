Our little story

It started with one sad office snack drawer

In 2021, our founders were tired of the same stale vending choices. So they began swapping homemade treats and discoveries from tiny makers. Friends wanted in. Friends-of-friends wanted in. Snackbloom is that snack drawer — grown up, curated with care, and delivered to thousands of doors.

We're a small team of taste-obsessed humans on a mission: make joy a monthly habit and put more money in the pockets of brilliant small makers.

Good to know

Questions, answered

Boxes ship in the first week of every month. Subscribe before the 25th and your first box catches the very next drop — otherwise it lands the month after.

Always. Skip an individual month, pause for a while, or cancel entirely from your dashboard in two clicks — no emails, no hoops, no hard feelings.

During sign-up you can flag nuts, gluten, dairy, soy and more. We tailor your curation around them and label every item clearly. (Items are made in shared facilities, so we can't guarantee zero traces.)

We currently ship to 17 countries with free domestic shipping and flat-rate international. Pop your postcode in at checkout to see your options.

The surprise is half the fun, so contents stay a secret — but your taste profile and monthly ratings shape every future box, so it keeps getting more "you".
