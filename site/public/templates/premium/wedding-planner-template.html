Our Philosophy

The Atelier

I am Aurelle — and I plan very few weddings, very well.

Sixteen years ago I planned a friend's wedding on a Tuscan hillside and never looked back. What began as one celebration grew into a discreet atelier trusted by couples across the world — yet the philosophy has never changed: take fewer clients, give them everything.

I am present at every meeting, every site visit, and every wedding day. You will never be handed to a coordinator you have not met. That intimacy is the whole point.

Aurelle Devereux

Founder & Lead Designer

How We Work

Good To Know

Questions, answered

How many weddings do you take each year?

+

A maximum of twelve. This deliberate ceiling lets us pour ourselves entirely into each couple — there is no overflow team, only us.

Do you travel for destination weddings?

+

Always — and joyfully. We have designed celebrations across twenty-seven countries, from Amalfi cliffs to Scottish glens. Travel and accommodation are quoted transparently within your proposal.

When should we book the atelier?

+

Most couples reach out twelve to eighteen months ahead. That said, we have worked beautiful magic in far shorter windows — do enquire regardless of your timeline.

Can you work with our existing suppliers?

+

Of course. Whether you arrive with a full list or a blank page, we adapt around what you already love and gently fill the gaps.
