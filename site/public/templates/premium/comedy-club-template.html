★ SINCE 2009 ★

HOW THE HECKLE STARTED

It began as a Tuesday-night dare in the back of a failing diner. One folding chair, one borrowed mic, one comic who refused to leave. Sixteen years later we've got real seats, a liquor license, and a marquee that only flickers when it's feeling dramatic. The dare never ended.
