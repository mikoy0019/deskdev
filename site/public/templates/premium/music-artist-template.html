FROM THE NIGHT SHIFT
WHO IS NOCTERRA

A band built
after dark

NOCTERRA is a four-piece collective that traded daylight for studio glow. What started as late-night jam sessions in a leaky warehouse became a sound — equal parts shimmer and grit, romance and rust.

They write about cities that never sleep, the static between radio stations, and the strange warmth of being awake while everyone else dreams. Velvet Static is their boldest statement yet.
