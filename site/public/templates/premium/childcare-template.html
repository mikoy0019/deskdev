Our Story

Growing curious, kind, confident kids since 2012

Sproutling began as one sunlit room and a simple belief: children learn best when they feel safe, seen, and free to play. Today our team of early-childhood educators nurtures hundreds of little ones across four bright, purpose-built rooms.

Good Questions

Parents often ask…

We’re open Monday to Friday, 7:30am to 5:30pm, year-round. We close only for major public holidays and two staff-training days each year (always announced well in advance).

Secure keypad entry, full CCTV coverage, daily health screenings, allergy-aware kitchen, and CPR/First-Aid-certified staff. Only verified pick-up contacts can collect a child.

Yes! A nutritionist-designed breakfast, hot lunch, and two snacks are freshly prepared on-site every day, with dairy-free, gluten-free, and allergy-safe options.

Our parent app sends real-time photos, meal and nap logs, and learning notes throughout the day, plus a friendly summary at pick-up from your child’s lead teacher.
