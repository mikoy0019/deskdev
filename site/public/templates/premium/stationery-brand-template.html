our little studio

Slow craft, on heavyweight stock.

We're a two-bench studio tucked above a bakery. Every design starts as a pencil sketch, gets letterpressed on cotton-rag paper, and is checked by hand before it ever reaches your desk. No rush, no filler — just paper we'd want to use ourselves.

  • ✦ Cotton-rag & FSC-certified papers only
  • ✦ Hand-numbered limited runs

Questions, answered

Our notebooks use 120gsm cotton-rag, fountain-pen friendly stock; cards are letterpressed on 300gsm cotton paper. All FSC-certified or recycled.

Foil monograms and personalised covers are made to order and ship within 3–5 business days. Standard items ship next day.

Yes — we ship worldwide with carbon-neutral courier options. Free domestic shipping on orders over $45.

Most covers in the Everyday line take standard A5 refills, so you keep the cover you love and just swap the inside.
