Our story

Born from a single candle on a winter evening

Emberhaus started in a small kitchen, melting wax in a borrowed pot because nothing on the shelf smelled like the home we remembered. We chased that feeling — warm, honest, a little nostalgic — and learned to pour it into wax.

Today we're a tiny studio of makers who still pour every candle by hand. We believe scent is memory, and that the right glow can turn an ordinary room into somewhere you want to stay.
