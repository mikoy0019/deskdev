OUR STORY

We were tired of habit apps that felt like homework.

So we built the opposite. Boop started as a tiny weekend project between two friends who couldn't keep a single routine going. We made the simplest, silliest, most satisfying way to show up — and somehow it worked.

No streak anxiety. No productivity guilt. Just one happy tap at a time, until the habits build themselves.
