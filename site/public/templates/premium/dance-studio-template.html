04 — The Studio

Where motion
becomes voice.

Kinētic started in a borrowed warehouse with a sprung floor and a single mirror. Twelve years later we're a full academy — but the ethos hasn't moved: dance is a language, and everyone deserves to speak it loudly.

Two studios, a recovery room, and a performance black-box. No egos on the floor, no minimum talent — just a room that demands your full presence and gives it right back.

08 — Questions

Before you move

Zero. Most of our students started with none. Every style has open-level and beginner classes, and the crew adapts each session to who's in the room.

Anything you can move and sweat in. Bare feet or socks for contemporary, clean trainers for hip-hop & breaking, heels (we have loaners) for heels class.

Yes — unlimited members can pause once per quarter for up to four weeks, no fee. Just message the front desk 48 hours ahead.

Adult classes are 16+. We also run a youth academy (ages 6–15) on weekend mornings — ask about the junior track.
