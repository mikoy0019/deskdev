The Sennen Collection

A quieter way to fill a room.

Sennen began as a single bench, planed by hand in a Cornish workshop. Today it is a full language of soft edges and visible grain — pieces designed to age gracefully, gather marks, and earn their place over decades rather than seasons.

  • Made to order in batches of twelve, never warehoused
  • Mortise-and-tenon joinery, zero visible fasteners
  • Finished only with plant oils and natural wax

Materials & craft

We choose materials that get better with touch.

01

Slow-grown hardwood

European oak, walnut and ash from FSC-certified forests, air-dried for stability before a single cut is made.

02

Honest joinery

Dovetails, dowels and floating tenons cut by makers, not machines alone — joints meant to outlive their owners.

03

Natural finishes

Hard-wax oils that let the wood breathe, develop patina, and be refreshed at home with a soft cloth.
