The Venue

A small room with an enormous heart.

Built in 1948 inside a former glassworks, The Larkspur Stage was never meant to be grand — it was meant to be close. No seat sits more than fourteen rows from the stage. We commission new work, pay our artists fairly, and keep a third of every house priced under forty dollars. For seventy-seven seasons we have believed that the most radical thing a theatre can do is let strangers sit together in the dark and feel something true.

In the round

A reconfigurable house — proscenium, thrust, or full surround for every production.

New work first

Nine of ten productions are world or regional premieres written for our stage.

Open doors

Pay-what-you-can previews and relaxed performances every run, no questions asked.
