The Shop

A room that smells
like cardboard
and good taste.

We started in a back-alley garage with one milk-crate of 45s and a turntable held together with tape. Fifty years on, the crates run wall to wall and the coffee’s still free.

Every record is graded by hand, cleaned on our ultrasonic bath, and sleeved in fresh anti-static. If a needle has touched it, we’ve heard it.
