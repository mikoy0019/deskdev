Our Story

We opened Lustré to make beauty feel slow, intentional and quietly luxurious — a softly-lit refuge where craft meets calm.

What began as a single chair in a sunlit corner is now a nine-artist atelier devoted to clean formulas, custom design and the kind of detail you can feel. Every visit is a small ceremony — and every guest leaves a little more luminous.
