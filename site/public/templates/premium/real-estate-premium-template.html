02 — The Premise

A house should be argued for,
not advertised.

Three convictions hold the firm together. They decide who we represent, which doors we open, and the long silences we're willing to keep on a client's behalf.

Ⅰ

Scarcity, honestly

We carry few listings on purpose. Each one is one we'd sign our own name beneath. No volume, no padding — a deliberately short shelf.

Ⅱ

Quiet by default

Discretion is the product. Off-market first, signage never, and a guest list rather than an open house. Your move stays your own.

Ⅲ

Taste, defended

We read floorplans like manuscripts — light, proportion, the walk from door to window. We'll talk you out of the wrong one.

04 — The House

Two desks, a single standard.

We began in 1998 above a framer's shop on Holloway Road with a rule we still keep: never carry a home we couldn't defend in conversation. The market grew loud. We grew quieter.

Today the firm is still small by design — a handful of principals, a closed network of architects, restorers and notaries, and a register that travels by introduction rather than advertisement. The work is slow because the work is personal.

06 — The Method

Four movements.

01

The conversation

Before a single floorplan, we sit. What you want from a home is rarely what a brief says. We listen for the unsaid line — then build the search around it.

02

The shortlist

We open the off-market register and our architect network. You'll see few homes — each one argued for in writing, with its flaws named plainly.

03

The walk

Private viewings, at the hour the house is at its best. We test the light, the silence, the walk from door to window — together, unhurried.

04

The close

Negotiation, notaries and the quiet logistics of acquisition — handled end to end. You receive keys; you never receive the noise.

07 — Enquiries

Before you
ask.

If your question isn't here, write to us. We answer every note personally, usually within the day.

Do you publish your listings publicly?

+

Rarely. The majority of what we carry is off-market and shared by introduction. The handful shown here is the public edge of a much longer register.

What is your minimum engagement?

+

We represent acquisitions and sales typically from £2.5M. Below that we're happy to refer you to colleagues we trust completely.

Do you work outside the cities shown?

+

Yes. London, the Côte d'Azur and the American West are our home ground, but our architect network reaches considerably further. Ask.

How private is the process, really?

+

Entirely. No signage, no portals, no leaks. NDAs are standard, viewings are by guest list, and your name never appears in our marketing — because we don't market you.
