Before you go

Good questions.

Every trip is graded one to five. Most travelers comfortably handle grades one to three with regular weekend walks. We send a tailored training plan twelve weeks out so you arrive ready.

Around half of every group books solo. With a twelve-person cap you'll know everyone by day two. There's no forced single supplement — we pair same-gender shares unless you'd like a private room.

Permits, in-country transfers, expert guiding, technical group gear, lodging and most meals. You cover flights, travel insurance and a few free evenings. No surprise upsells on the trail — ever.

Cancel up to sixty days out for a full credit toward any future trip. Inside sixty days our flexible policy and recommended insurance have you covered for the unexpected.
