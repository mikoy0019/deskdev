Frequently asked

How fast is delivery, really?

+

Our city average is 27 minutes door-to-door. Most orders within 2km land in under 20. You'll see a live estimate before you check out.

Is there a delivery fee?

+

Fees start at $1.99 and vary by distance and demand. Forkly+ members get unlimited free delivery on orders over $15.

What if my order is wrong or late?

+

Tap "Get help" in the app and our team responds in under 2 minutes. We refund or re-send fast — no long forms, ever.

How do I become a partner restaurant?

+

Hit "Become a partner" above. We'll verify your kitchen, ship a free tablet, and get you live — usually within 3 business days.
