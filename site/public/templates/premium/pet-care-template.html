Started by a dog named Biscuit

When our founder's senior beagle stopped eating, no shelf could help. So we partnered with veterinary nutritionists to cook real food in real kitchens — and Biscuit's appetite (and zoomies) came roaring back.

Today Pawmel feeds thousands of pets the way we fed Biscuit: with named ingredients, gentle cooking, and a vet you can actually talk to.
