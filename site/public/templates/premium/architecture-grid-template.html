(01) — Philosophy

We design slowly. Each project begins with the quality of a single room — the way morning falls across stone, the weight of a door, the silence a wall can hold — and grows outward into a building.

Material honestyConcrete, oak, lime and brass left to age, never disguised.

Proportion firstGeometry resolved before ornament. Restraint as a discipline.

Light as materialApertures designed around the path of the sun, season to season.

(04) — The Studio

Seventeen people. One conviction: that good space changes how a life is lived.

Founded in a former tile works in Lisbon, Meridian Atelier is an architecture and interior practice working between dwelling, culture and landscape. We keep our practice small so every drawing passes through a principal's hand.

What we do

(07) — Method

How we work

Four movements, repeated patiently on every commission — from a first conversation to the moment we hand over the keys.

01

Listen

We spend time in the place and with the people who'll inhabit it. The brief is written together.

02

Draw

Plans, sections and physical models in parallel — the idea tested in light, scale and material early.

03

Refine

Detailing down to the join. We prototype hardware, finishes and joinery before a wall is built.

04

Build

On site weekly. We see the project through construction so the drawing and the room agree.

(08) — Enquiries

Good to know

Yes. Roughly a third of our work is international. We establish a local site team and travel for every key milestone, so distance never dilutes the detail.

From a single considered room to a 4,000m² cultural building. We care less about scale than about ambition and the chance to do something quietly excellent.

Interiors run six to twelve months; a new-build residence eighteen to thirty. We share a clear phased timeline after our first studio visit.

We prefer to. Holding architecture, interiors and bespoke pieces under one roof is how a space achieves coherence — but we're glad to collaborate with a client's own makers.
