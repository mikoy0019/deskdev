The Atelier

Forty hands. One obsession.

Behind every Aurélise piece is a bench, a loupe, and an artisan who has spent decades perfecting a single craft. Setters, polishers, lapidaries — each touches the metal in turn, a silent relay passed down through four generations.

A single bespoke ring can take 300 hours. We would not have it any other way.
