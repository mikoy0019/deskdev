The Origin

BORN IN A
PARKING LOT.

It started with one beat-up truck, a luchador mask hanging off the mirror, and abuela's salsa recipe scribbled on a napkin. We grill loud, season louder, and never apologize for the mess.

Every tortilla is pressed in-house. Every cut of meat is wood-smoked low and slow before it ever hits the flat-top. No shortcuts, no freezer magic — just honest street food worth chasing across town.
