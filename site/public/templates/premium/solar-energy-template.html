Questions

Everything you were about to ask.

Most SolHaven homeowners offset 70–100% of their electricity, saving $30,000–$60,000 over 25 years depending on roof, usage and local rates. Your custom quote shows your exact projected savings.

Panels still produce in cloud cover, just less. At night your home draws from the grid — or from a Vault battery if you add one. Net metering credits the surplus you send back during the day.

The physical install is usually a single day. From signed quote to switched-on system typically takes 3–6 weeks, most of which is permitting and utility approval — all handled by us.

Panels, inverters and battery carry 25-year product and performance warranties, plus our 25-year workmanship and roof-penetration guarantee. If production ever dips, our monitoring flags it and we fix it free.

Most roofs are a great fit. Our free design checks orientation, shading, age and structure using satellite data. If your roof isn't ready, we'll tell you honestly — no obligation.
