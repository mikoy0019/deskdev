Our Story

BUILT ON STEEL,
LEATHER & STEAM

Ironhide & Co. started in a single rented chair behind a hardware store in 2009. Marcus Durant had two clippers, a worn strop, and a belief that a man's grooming should feel like a ritual — not a transaction.

Sixteen years later we run nine chairs of polished brass and oxblood leather. The philosophy hasn't moved an inch: take your time, get the lines right, send a man back into the world sharper than he came in.

"We don't rush the blade. Good work shows."
