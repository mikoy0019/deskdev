Good to know

Questions, answered

Is Hearthly free for home-seekers?

Yes. Searching, saving, alerts, and tour booking are completely free. We earn from optional agent and lender partnerships — never from your data.

How fresh are the listings?

We sync directly with MLS feeds and partner brokerages, refreshing every few minutes. Many homes appear on Hearthly before they reach the larger portals.

Can I bring my own agent?

Absolutely. Invite your agent to your Hearthly workspace and they'll see your saves, tours, and timeline. Prefer a match? We'll connect you with a vetted local pro.

Which cities are live?

We're live in 240+ metros across the US and adding new markets monthly. Drop your ZIP at sign-up and we'll tell you instantly — or notify you the day we launch nearby.
