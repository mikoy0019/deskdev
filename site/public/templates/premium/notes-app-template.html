Features

Everything you need to think clearly — and nothing you don't.

✍️

Frictionless capture

Open, type, done. A keystroke gets you to a blank page. Markdown, slash-commands, and clean defaults stay out of your way.

🔗

Bi-directional links

Mention any note with [[brackets]] and Loomwell wires the connection both ways — automatically.

🕸️

Living knowledge graph

Watch ideas cluster as you write. The graph reveals connections you'd never have noticed on your own.

🔍

Instant recall

Full-text, fuzzy, and semantic search find the half-remembered thought in milliseconds. Nothing gets lost.

🔒

Private by design

End-to-end encryption and local-first storage. Your thinking is yours — we genuinely can't read it.

🌙

Calm by default

No streaks, no badges, no dopamine traps. Typographic focus mode and warm tones designed to lower the noise.

How it works
