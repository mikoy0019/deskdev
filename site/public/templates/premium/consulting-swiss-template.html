03 — The Method

A way of
thinking.

“We do not sell decks. We sell judgment — and the conviction to defend it.”

— Founding Principle, Article I

01

Frame the real question

Most mandates arrive mis-stated. We spend the first week dismantling the question until only the decision that matters remains.

02

Evidence over instinct

Primary data, proprietary models, and field diligence. We pressure-test every assumption until it earns its place in the recommendation.

03

A single defensible path

No menu of options designed to avoid accountability. One recommendation, the reasoning laid bare, and a partner who will stand behind it.

04

Stay until it holds

Advice without implementation is theatre. We remain through execution — to the moment the decision proves itself in the numbers.

04 — The Firm

Founded on the belief that good advice is rare, and that scarcity is the point.

We keep the firm deliberately small. Fewer partners, fewer clients, and the freedom to refuse work that does not deserve our full attention.

i.

Independence

No banking arm, no products to sell, no incentive but the quality of the counsel itself.

ii.

Discretion

The most important work is never announced. Our reference list is private, and our clients prefer it that way.

iii.

Continuity

The partner who wins the mandate is the partner who delivers it — from first meeting to final board.

07 — Enquiries

Before you
engage.

Retainer for strategic mandates, success-linked for transactions. We agree the basis in writing before any work begins, and we never bill for a recommendation we would not act on ourselves.

Always. A named partner owns your mandate end to end. We do not operate a pyramid, and you will never be handed to a team you have not met.

Frequently. If we cannot add judgment beyond what you already hold, or if a conflict exists, we say so at the outset. Saying no is part of the service.

Strategy mandates run six to fourteen weeks; transactions, longer. Where it matters, we stay through implementation rather than handing over a document and departing.
