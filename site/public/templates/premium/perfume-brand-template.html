The House

Composed in shadow,
worn in light.

MAISON NÖIR began in a small atelier above the flower fields of Grasse, where our founding nose refused the industrial pace of modern perfumery. No briefs. No focus groups. Only the slow conversation between raw material and intuition.

Every formula is macerated for months, hand-bottled, and numbered. We make fragrance the way it was made a century ago — patiently, and without apology.

Questions

Before you choose

As true extraits de parfum (25–30% concentration), our fragrances are designed to last 10–14 hours on skin, with a soft sillage that stays close and intimate.

Our compositions are 100% natural origin, blending single-source absolutes, essential oils, and sustainably harvested resins. We never use animal-derived materials.

Yes — we ship to over 60 countries with tracked, carbon-neutral delivery. Discovery sets ship within 48 hours; full bottles are dispatched within five business days.
