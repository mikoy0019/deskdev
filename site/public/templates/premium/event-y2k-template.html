HYPERDRIVE 2099 is what the millennium dreamed it would become. We took the optimism of the year 2000 — the chrome, the gel, the impossible blue — and cranked it through forty subwoofers.

Three nights inside the Aurora Dome. Holographic stages, a zero-gravity chillout pod, and a crowd of 12,000 dressed like the cover of a CD-ROM. This isn't a throwback. It's a rerun of the future.

THE FAQ

HYPERDRIVE 2099 is strictly 18+. Bring a valid government photo ID — the chrome gate scanners will check it and there are no exceptions.
