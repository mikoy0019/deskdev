The capabilities

Engineered to feel like intuition.

01 — PRESENCE

A computer made of light, not screens.

HALO projects an ambient field that reads your space and your attention. No glass to stare into — just a presence that brightens when you need it and dissolves when you don't.

  • Volumetric projection up to 3 meters
  • Gaze + gesture aware
02 — ANTICIPATION

It prepares the moment before you ask.

An on-device 4B neural core learns your rhythms locally — surfacing the call, the recipe, the route, or the light you wanted, a beat before the thought finishes.

  • Predictive context engine
  • Nothing leaves the device
03 — CONSTELLATION

One HALO is calm. A room of them is magic.

Place several and they self-organize into a mesh — handing your context room to room, so the light always knows where you are and what you meant.

  • Zero-config mesh pairing
  • Seamless context hand-off

The origin

We were tired of computers that demanded our eyes.

Aurelius began in a dim studio in 2024 with a stubborn belief: technology should recede into the room, not dominate it. Five years and eleven prototypes later, HALO is light you can talk to — built by a team of physicists, animators, and one very patient acoustician.

Before you reserve

Questions, answered.

Does HALO send my data to the cloud?

+

Never by default. The neural core runs entirely on-device. Cloud sync is optional, encrypted, and off until you explicitly enable it.

When does the deposit charge and when does it ship?

+

Your $99 deposit is collected today and is fully refundable any time before shipment. Generation Zero units ship in waves through winter 2026.

Do I need multiple units?

+

No — a single HALO is complete. Additional units simply form a mesh, handing your context from room to room as you move.

Is it repairable?

+

Yes. HALO is built modular — the core, optics, and battery are individually replaceable, with parts and guides available to everyone.
