About

I'm Lume — a maker of quiet, cinematic images.

Based between Lisbon and wherever the next assignment lands, I work in available light and patient composition. My background in film photography taught me restraint — to wait, to underexpose, to let mood do the talking.

From editorial spreads to private portrait commissions, every frame is treated as a single, deliberate sentence in a longer story. No noise. No filler. Only what the moment offered.
