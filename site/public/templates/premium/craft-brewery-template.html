Grain to Glass

The Brewing Process

01

Mash & Mill

We crack whole-grain malt and steep it warm to coax out fermentable sugars. The first sweet smell of the day.

02

Boil & Hop

A rolling boil, then hops added in waves for bitterness, flavor, and aroma. Timing is everything.

03

Ferment

House yeast does the quiet work — two weeks of patience turning sweet wort into proper beer.

04

Condition & Pour

Cold-conditioned, lightly carbonated, and racked straight to the taproom lines. Fresh as it gets.

Since 2014

Built in an
Old Foundry

Ironhollow started with two friends, one cobbled-together kettle, and a stubborn belief that the neighborhood deserved better beer. We poured our first pint to a room of twelve people. We've kept the same crew, the same fire, and the same rule: brew what we'd want to drink.

A decade later we're still independent, still small-batch, and still answering to nobody but the regulars who pull up a stool.
