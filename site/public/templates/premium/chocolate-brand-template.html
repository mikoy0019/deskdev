The Ritual

How to taste a great bar

Slow down. Let the chocolate melt on the tongue rather than chewing — the flavor unfolds in waves.

  • ✦Listen for the snap — a sign of proper temper.
  • ✦Warm a square between your fingers, then inhale.
  • ✦Let it melt; note the first, middle, and finish.

Our Story

Founded in a tiny workshop, by two who couldn't find a bar they loved.

Cacaoluna began with a borrowed melanger and a stubborn belief that chocolate should taste of the place it came from. Today we still make every batch by hand, in the same workshop, one origin at a time.
