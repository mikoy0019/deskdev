The problem we exist for

A child shouldn't walk six hours for a drink that makes her sick.

In the villages we serve, the nearest water is often a contaminated stream — and the long walk to fetch it steals the hours a child should spend in school. Water and education are not two problems. They are one. Solve the well, and the classroom fills.

Water that lasts

Solar-pumped wells maintained by trained local technicians — not abandoned in a year.

Education that follows

When water is close, attendance climbs. We build the classroom while the well is drilled.
