The Distillery

Fire, Copper & Time

  1. 01

    Malting & Mash

    Floor-malted Highland barley, kilned over local peat and beechwood for a layered smoke.

  2. 02

    Copper Distillation

    Twice through hand-beaten copper stills, cut narrow for a cleaner, fuller spirit.

  3. 03

    The Char

    Casks charred by hand to a deep alligator finish — the ember that names us.

  4. 04

    Patient Rest

    Years in cool stone warehouses where Highland air does the slow, quiet work.

Since 1987

A family of three generations
tending one fire.

Cask & Ember was founded on a single belief: that whiskey worth keeping cannot be rushed. From a stone barn on the edge of the glen, founder Eilidh MacArran lit the first kiln and laid down forty casks. Today her grandchildren keep the same flame — same water, same wood, same refusal to compromise.

Spring-Fed Water

Drawn from the same glen spring since the first batch.

Hand-Built Casks

Family-Owned

On the Palate

Tasting Notes

Nose

Warm toffee and charred oak rise first, then orchard fruit and a thread of distant peat smoke.

Palate

Dark honey, dried fig and baking spice coat the tongue, balanced by a clean copper minerality.

Finish

Long and glowing — embers of smoke, bittersweet cocoa and a final lick of sea salt.
