The Room

A room lit only
by the fire.

No overhead lights. No noise but the murmur of the room and the snap of oak. We seat slowly, pour generously, and let the evening find its own pace.

Cellar of 200 labels. A bar that smokes its own ice. Tables tucked into alcoves, built for the long conversation.

The Chef

Marcus Cordell

Marcus learned fire in his grandfather's yard before he ever stood in a kitchen. Twenty years later — through embered grills in Buenos Aires and a Michelin pass in London — he came home to build the room he always wanted to eat in.

"A steak is honest. You can't hide behind it. Either you respected the fire, or you didn't."

— Marcus Cordell, Executive Chef
