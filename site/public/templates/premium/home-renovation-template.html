Our story

A family trade, sharpened for modern homes.

Hearthstone & Co. started in 2009 when two brothers — a cabinetmaker and a project manager — got tired of watching homeowners get burned by remodels that ran double the quote. They built a company around one rule: the price we shake on is the price you pay.

Seventeen years and 640 rooms later, that rule still holds. We're a tight team of master carpenters, tile-setters, and one slightly obsessive designer who all live within twenty minutes of the homes we work on.
