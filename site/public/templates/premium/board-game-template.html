The World of the Hollow

Long ago, the Old Lantern kept Mosswick Hollow in endless autumn — warm, golden, and forever harvest-time. But the lantern's flame is dimming, and the Thornwardens stir beneath the brambles.

Five critter clans must set aside old grudges (mostly) and gather enough acorns to relight the flame — before the First Frost creeps in and the Hollow sleeps forever.
