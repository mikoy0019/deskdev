The Saltwave Story

It started with one busted longboard and a stubborn refusal to leave the water.

In 2014, two friends started shaping boards in a borrowed garage three streets back from the break. No investors, no marketing plan — just sawdust, salt, and the belief that a board should feel like an extension of you. A decade later we still glass every board by hand, and we still surf before we ship.

A small crew with one big rule: surf first.
